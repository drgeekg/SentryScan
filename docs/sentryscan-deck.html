01 · What it is

Two products and a demo rig on one agent core.

Executive Portal /POLARIS continuous compliance + SENTRA sensitive-data discovery, AI SOC copilot, executive posture reports and in-browser PDFs. 11-asset inventory.
Master Console /masterVIGIL LangGraph multi-agent SOC: live agent topology, Contract-Net auctions, BDI deliberation, dry-run containment playbooks.
Control Center /controlHold/resume simulation, inject breaches, reset baseline, isolate assets. Sub-50 ms remediation demo.
